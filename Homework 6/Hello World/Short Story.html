Funny Story


I don't know how funny this will be to a stranger reading this, but it was funny when it happened to me. (this is a true story)
So basically, I was watching this television show on a streaming service like any normal human being does. It was a good show with good plot, pacing, and diverse cast of characters. There were these two characters that had great chemistry and they start dating, as normal people do when romance or whatever. Fast forward a little bit and the characters share their first kiss. It was a sweet moment that was VERY ABRUPTLY interuppted by a DoorDash commercial right after it happened. I'm not sure why, but I found this to be so hilarious that I couldn't stop laughing for a solid two minutes after that. I don't know if the streaming service could pick where the ads were placed within episodes, but this was the absolute worst timing they could have picked. I still reference this incident to this day with my siblings and it is funny every time. If you don't think this is funny, put yourself in my shoes. You just witnessed a beautiful moment unfold before your eyes on the screen. You are comepletely invested in the moment, and then, "ARE YOU TOO LAZY TO ACTUALLY COOK, ORDER SOME DOORDASH TODAY FOR THE LOW PRICE OF 50 DOLLARS FOR A SINGLE MEAL". The contrast between the personal moment and the generic commercial was just comedy gold. That's about it, see ya.
